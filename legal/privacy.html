Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-19

Short version: Cartridge has no ads, no analytics, no tracking SDKs and does not sell or share your data. We store only what the features you use need.

Without an account

  • The app downloads the game list and game files from GitHub Pages (4gus71n.github.io). As with any website, GitHub sees your IP address when you download; see GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Kept on your device only: installed games, which games you've already seen, your settings, and each game's own save data (high scores, progress). Clearing the app's data or uninstalling removes all of it.
  • If you report a game, we store the report (reason and optional details), with no identity attached.

With an account

We store on our server:

  • your email address and a salted hash of your password (never the password itself);
  • your likes, saves, collections and comments;
  • reports you send;
  • if you submit games, your creator display name, the terms version you accepted, and the game details and links you submitted.

Your email is never shown to other players. Comments show the part of your email before the "@", and creators show the display name they chose.

We use this data only to run these features. It is never used for advertising and never sold.

Where it lives

The server runs on infrastructure we control. Traffic reaches it through Cloudflare (a tunnel provider), which handles the encrypted connection and sees IP addresses. Database backups are kept for at most 14 days.

Deleting your data

In the app: Profile → Delete account. This immediately and permanently deletes your email, password hash, likes, saves, collections, comments and creator profile. Reports you sent stay, with no identity attached, so moderation history isn't lost. Games you published stay available under the license you released them with, no longer linked to you. Backups age out within 14 days.

You can also ask us to delete or export your data via the contact page.

Children

Cartridge is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. If a change is significant, we'll also announce it in the app.
