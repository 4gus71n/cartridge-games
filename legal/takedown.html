Contact & takedown requests

Report a game

Use ⋯ → Report inside any game. You don't need an account. Reports reach the moderation queue straight away.

Copyright and rights claims (DMCA-style)

If you believe a game on Cartridge uses your work without permission, send us:

  1. the game's name (and a link to it, if you have one);
  2. what work of yours it uses, and where it's published;
  3. how we can reach you;
  4. a statement that you believe in good faith the use isn't authorised, and that the information you sent is accurate.

We act promptly. We usually remove the game from the catalog within a few days, and the app then deletes its downloaded copy from players' devices. We'll tell the creator, who can respond.

How to reach us

Open an issue at https://github.com/4gus71n/cartridge-games/issues

Data requests

To get a copy of your data, or to have it deleted without using the in-app button, reach us the same way. Don't post personal details in public issues: just ask us for a private channel.
